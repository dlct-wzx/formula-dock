# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple standard of respectful, constructive collaboration.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing experience levels, languages, and viewpoints.
- Give actionable technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Assume good faith while requiring evidence for security and privacy claims.
- Respect user data and never request or publish credentials, private images, or personal information.
- Keep issue and pull-request discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private data or credentials.
- Deliberately introducing malware, credential theft, hidden telemetry, or excessive permissions.
- Disrupting discussions or repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when behaviour violates this policy. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and documented privately where appropriate.

Report sensitive conduct or security concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's available private reporting channel.
